The first image shows four characters of a children's game about a school of magic, in its style: two first-year pupils, a woman teacher and a man teacher. The second image is a portrait of another character of the game, Orla, the head of the school. Draw Orla's whole figure in exactly the style of the first image and with exactly the proportions of its grown-ups, chibi: a big head on a short body, as tall as the woman teacher shown: an older woman, calm and kind, a gentle wrinkled face, grey hair gathered in a neat bun, light skin, small round spectacles, a long deep blue robe with a high collar trimmed in silver, a silver brooch shaped like a star, and dark shoes; she wears no hat. She is the very woman of the portrait.

Draw four figures of her in one row, evenly spaced, each alone, with wide empty gaps between them, on a flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background. First figure: facing the viewer. Second figure: facing to the right, seen from the side. Third figure: facing to the left, seen from the side. Fourth figure: her back to the viewer. In every figure she stands with her feet together, side by side, both flat on the ground and her weight on both, no step, no foot forward and no foot in the air; her hands folded calmly in front of her. Every figure the same size, full body with the feet, standing on the same baseline; seen in the game's three-quarter top-down view, as in the first image. No shadows, no ground, no text, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames. Style: painted cartoon 2D game art, exactly the style of the first image: bold dark outlines, warm saturated colours, soft painted shading, chunky readable shapes.
