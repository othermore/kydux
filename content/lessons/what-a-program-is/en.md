## A list of instructions

A computer does only what it is told.
So we tell it what to do, one step at a time.

Each step is an **instruction**.
A list of instructions is a **program**.

The computer does the instructions in order.
It starts at the top, and goes down, one line after another.

---

## An instruction for the wizard

Your program tells the wizard what to do.
The wizard does exactly what it says: no more, and no less.

Here is one instruction:

```python
wizard.move_right()
```

It makes the wizard take one step to the right.
In the next lesson you will see what each part of it means.
