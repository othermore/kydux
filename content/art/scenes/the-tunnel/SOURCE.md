# The corridor of doors and the tunnel

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-tunnel-corridor.md`, then edited by it without its
  grid (`game-tunnel-nogrid.md`).
- What it left black round the corridor painted by Gemini from
  `game-tunnel-voids.md`, joined by `tools/art/repaint.py`.
- What stayed black at its top and left tiled from the picture's own stones
  (`tiled`), the small black marks behind its torches covered with the wall's
  own stretch above them (`placed`), and the streaks on its walls filled from
  the pixels beside them (`fill`), no model asked.
