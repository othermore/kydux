# The welcome page's pictures

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later, like the
rest of it.

- `welcome-castle.webp`: Arvelia on its hill above the lake at dusk, with the village at
  its foot, seen from the field's path. Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image
  (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from `tools/art/prompts/welcome-castle.md`, with the style of
  the game's painting of the field, and made 1600 pixels wide.
- `<lang>/story.webp`, `<lang>/lesson.webp`, `<lang>/level.webp`: the game itself, in
  each language, taken by the browser at 1280 by 800 and made 960 pixels wide: the
  fourth step of *The morning after*, the first page of the lesson on lists, and *The
  first flight* while the wizard flies over the water.
