# Drevo

The magic word `drevo` lifts the broom you carry into the air! You cast it with your wand, writing `wizard.cast("drevo")`.

From then on, your usual moves fly! Flying crosses water, hedges, fences and benches, and it goes three times as fast as walking. But be careful: a flight does not go through the crowns of the trees nor through walls. The broom simply sets down wherever your program ends.
