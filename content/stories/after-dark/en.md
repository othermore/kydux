# After dark

It is night in the castle. You cannot sleep. There is a soft knock at the door. Nuri peeks in. "Are you awake, {wizard}?"

---

"The older pupils say the passages change at night," Nuri whispers. "Dask patrols the halls. He does not want anyone out of bed." Nuri smiles. "Let us go see."

---

You tiptoe into the dark. Nuri knows the way to the watchtower gallery. Nuri teaches you a new rule of magic. You must look before you step.

---

"We must get past Dask," Nuri says. "We must ask a question with a true or false answer. Programmers call this question an expression. Let me show you how."
