# The top of the stairs

You reach the top of the long stairs. The night passages begin here. The stones are older and darker.

---

A stair floats in the air ahead. It comes and goes. You hear footsteps somewhere below. It is Dask.

---

"We must be careful here," Nuri whispers. "We have to wait for the right moment. We need to repeat an action while something stays true."
