# Each thing its methods

Until now, every method you used was your wizard's: `wizard.move_up()`, `wizard.pick_up()`. What stands before the dot is the object that does it; what stands after is what it does. But your wizard is not the only object in the castle: each object has methods of its own!

---
## The door

This door before us is an object. The program already keeps it in a variable, `door`, just as it keeps your wizard in `wizard`. It has a method of its own: `door.open()`. For the door to open, your wizard must stand beside it.

---
## Whose method is it?

If you write `wizard.open()`, it will not work. Why? Because opening is something the door does, not your wizard, so the method is written after the door's variable, `door`.

The notebook shows each object with its own methods. When in doubt about whose a method is, look there! Now, let us open this door.
