# Notes

We want to leave a note in our program to remember something, to explain what it does, or to write down something we still have to do.

Programmers call this kind of note a comment. A comment is for a person reading the program, not for the computer.

---
## How to write a comment

To write a comment, you start the line with the `#` sign. Anything you write after that sign on the same line is part of the comment.

When the computer runs your program and sees a comment, it simply skips it!

---
## Comments in Arvelia

In this game, the comments you find in a level's program are there to help you. They will tell you what to do to solve the puzzle and move forward!
