# The crossing

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-tunnel-exit.md`, then edited by it without its
  grid (`game-crossing-nogrid.md`).
- The corridor beside the statues made three cells wide by
  `tools/art/repaint.py spaced`.
- The pale paper round the castle painted as its inside by Gemini from
  `game-crossing-voids.md` and `game-crossing-voids-more.md`, joined by
  `tools/art/repaint.py`, and what still was black tiled from the picture's
  own stones (`tiled`); `game-crossing-voids-top.md` was asked first and kept
  the paper as the page.
- Its statues and its low arch cut by the polygons traced round them.
