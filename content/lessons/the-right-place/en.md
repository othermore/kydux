# The right place

You must be very tired after your journey! But even if you are sleepy, you cannot just sleep anywhere. Some actions can only be done in the right place.

---
## Where to do an action

In this game, the method `wizard.sleep()` lays your wizard down in a free bed, but only when the wizard stands beside the trunk at the foot of that bed. If you try to sleep anywhere else, your wizard will wonder why you asked, and nothing will happen!

So, a program must first walk to the right place, and then do the action.

---
## Try it

You are in your own dormitory, between the beds, but beside no bed's trunk. Let us see what happens if we try to sleep right here!

wizard.sleep()
