# The storeroom

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-dark-room.md`, then edited by it without its grid
  (`game-storeroom-nogrid.md`).
- The dark bands round it painted by Gemini from `game-storeroom-voids.md`,
  joined by `tools/art/repaint.py`, and what still was dark tiled from its
  own stones (`tiled`).
- The key taken out by `tools/art/repaint.py fill`, and cut by the polygon
  traced round it.
- The hole of the slab painted by Gemini from `game-storeroom-hole.md`, and laid
  at its cell's size by `tools/art/repaint.py placed`.
- The door opened by `tools/art/door.py` from the painting itself, no model asked.
