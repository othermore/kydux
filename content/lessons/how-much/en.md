# How far we move

Walking a long way by writing one step on each line is very slow. If you want to move six steps, you would have to write the same line six times! You might even write it five or seven times by mistake, or misspell one of them.

Programmers are lazy, but in a good way! They like to do things with the fewest instructions possible.

---
## Arguments

We can tell a move method exactly how far to go. We put a number inside its brackets, which programmers call an argument. If you leave the brackets empty with no number, it means one step.

Every method has its own arguments, which mean their own things, or sometimes none at all. A move's argument says how many steps to take.
