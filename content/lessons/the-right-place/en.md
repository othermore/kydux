# The right place

You must be very tired after your journey! But even if you are sleepy, you cannot just sleep anywhere. Some methods can only be used in the right place.

---
## Where to use a method

In this game, the method `wizard.sleep()` lays your wizard down in a free bed, but only when the wizard stands beside the trunk at the foot of that bed. If you try to sleep anywhere else, your wizard will not know why you used that method, and nothing will happen!

So, a program must first walk to the right place, and then use the method.
