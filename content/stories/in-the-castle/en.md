# In the castle

The great doors close behind us. The castle is warm and bright. Ilda, a teacher, smiles at the new pupils. "Welcome to Arvelia," she says. "You will learn great things here."

---

Ilda looks at your wand. "That is a spare wand, {wizard}. You must go to the wand bank to borrow a school wand for the year. Then you can give Nuri's wand back. The bank is on the other side of the library." Nuri waves goodbye and goes to class.

---

You walk to the library. The room is full of tall shelves and old books. Wenna, the librarian, stands by the door. She is quiet and loves a good question.

---

"The library is a place of order," Wenna says. "I will help you find your way across it. Before you reach the wand bank, there are new rules of magic to learn. I will teach them to you on the way. Let us begin."
