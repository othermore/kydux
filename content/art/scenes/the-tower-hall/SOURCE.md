# The tower's highest hall

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/game-tower-hall.md`, given its plan on the grid and the
  design's first painting of the hall (`tools/art/prompts/scene-tower-hall.md`),
  in the style of `art/raw/image-first/field-2k.png`.
- Edited by it: its grid taken out and the perches and baskets that stood on
  the walked floor taken away (`tower-hall-base.md`); the cat moved onto the
  lectern (`tower-hall-mend.md`) and the three plates onto their cells
  (`tower-hall-moon.md`, `tower-hall-sun.md`, `tower-hall-star.md`), each
  mended in its box by `tools/art/repaint.py`, the painting kept elsewhere.
- Its lit plates (`tower-hall-lit.md`), its light at sunset
  (`tower-hall-sunset.md`) and its archway marked for the cut
  (`tower-hall-arch-marked-2.md`) are Gemini's edits of that painting.
- The sun's and the star's engravings made as dark as the moon's by it
  (`tower-hall-sun-engraving.md`, `tower-hall-star-engraving.md`), and at
  sunset the same (`tower-hall-sun-engraving-sunset.md`,
  `tower-hall-star-engraving-sunset.md`, the sun given the day's as its
  model), each joined by `tools/art/repaint.py` in its plate's outline.
- The familiars' tunnel's wall is cut from the painting by
  `tools/art/export_scene.py`, a polygon round its mouth less the opening.
