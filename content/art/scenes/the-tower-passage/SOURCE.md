# The small passage

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-small-passage.md`, in the style of
  `art/raw/image-first/field-2k.png`, and edited by it
  (`scene-small-passage-2.md`, `scene-small-passage-3.md`).
- Its trench painted by it on an enlargement of the wall
  (`scene-small-passage-6.md`), squeezed onto the middles of its cells by
  `tools/art/narrow.py`, repaired by it (`scene-small-passage-7.md`), its
  rubble painted again (`scene-small-passage-8.md`), and laid back by
  `tools/art/narrow.py`.
- Its grid taken out by it (`game-tower-passage-nogrid.md`).
- Its second flap painted by it on a part of the corridor's wall
  (`tower-passage-flap.md`) and laid on the painting by `tools/art/lay.py`,
  only its own pixels.
- The forgotten room's side door open painted by Gemini for the game's
  owner on a part of the painting, the wall without the door and the leaf
  alone, the leaf set in its place by the owner; laid on the painting by
  `tools/art/lay.py`, its doorway and its leaf, only their own pixels, the
  leaf a little darker.
- The corridor's arch's wall is cut from the painting by
  `tools/art/export_scene.py`, a polygon round it less its opening.
