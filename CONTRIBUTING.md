# Contributing to Kydux

Kydux's code, levels, texts and art are free software under the GNU
General Public License, version 3 or later (`LICENSE`). A change you send,
a pull request or a patch, is published under the same licence, as the GPL
asks, and you keep the copyright of it.

So by sending a change to Kydux, you agree that:

1. The change is your own work, or you have the right to give it, and it
   carries nothing under a licence the GPL cannot take in. A picture, a
   sound or a font from anywhere else comes only under a licence that lets
   it ship with a GPL game (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, the GPL, the OFL for a
   font), with its origin, its author and its licence written beside it.
2. You license your change under the GNU General Public License, version
   3 or later, as the rest of Kydux is.
3. You keep the copyright of your change, and may use it elsewhere as you
   like.

Code, comments and commit messages are in English. Whatever a player
reads comes from the translation files, in English and in Spanish, with
the same keys in each. `ci/check.sh` runs every check of the project; run
it before you send a change.

How to set up a computer for the work is in `docs/dev/dev-environment.md`,
and how each part of the game works in the other documents of `docs/dev/`,
which `docs/dev/README.md` lists.
