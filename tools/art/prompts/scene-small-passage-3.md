Finish this picture. It is one scene of a children's game, seen from above: a corridor across the bottom, a thick mass of old masonry across the middle, and a small forgotten room at the top. Three flat colours have been laid over it as instructions: flat GREY patches, one bright GREEN line and two bright RED squares. None of the three colours may remain in the finished picture. Everything that is not under those colours stays exactly as it is: the style, the view, the light, the faint light grid, the rooms, the big archway with steps at the left, the bench, the tapestry.

THE GREEN LINE is a very narrow little tunnel for small animals, a fox cub or a cat, cut into the masonry as a sunken trench with its lid lifted off. Paint it exactly where the green line is and exactly as narrow as the green line is, no wider: a floor of tiny dusty paving stones well below the top of the masonry, its two sides in shadow, right-angled corners, a cobweb, a few pebbles. It is far too narrow for a child, even crawling.

THE TWO RED SQUARES are the two ends of that tunnel: each a tiny arched opening like a cat flap neatly built in stone, exactly as small as its red square, dark inside, sitting at the very foot of its wall's front face. The lower one is at the foot of the corridor's wall, on the corridor's floor, and it is lower than the seat of the bench. The upper one is at the foot of the wall under the small room, where the green line ends.

THE GREY PATCHES are to be painted over with what surrounds them, so that no trace of a patch is left: in the masonry, the same big rough grey stone blocks and rubble seen from above as at both sides, closing in tight against the narrow tunnel; in the two walls, the same plain front face of fitted stone blocks and the same lit stone top as the rest of each wall, running on unbroken; where a patch reaches the small room's floor, that same dusty floor. There is no big arch, no wide trench and no kerb of big stones anywhere in the masonry: only the narrow tunnel of the green line.

No people, no animals and no text anywhere.
