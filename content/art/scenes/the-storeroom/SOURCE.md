# The storeroom

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-dark-room.md`, then edited by it: without its grid
  (`game-storeroom-nogrid.md`), the round of the castle painted
  (`game-storeroom-voids.md`), the hole in the floor (`game-storeroom-hole.md`).
- Its base is Gemini's edit without the door (`storeroom-base.md`); the door open is its
  edit of the base (`storeroom-open.md`), and each leaf the difference (D148). So are the
  arches, from `storeroom-gone.md`.
