# The dormitory

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/image-first-dormitory-3.md`, with the field as its
  style's reference; its border and the wands on its trunks edited by it
  (`game-dormitory-border.md`), its grid removed (`game-dormitory-nogrid.md`).
- Its corridor's right end mended by Gemini, joined by `tools/art/repaint.py`,
  the painting kept pixel for pixel elsewhere: the castle's wall closing the
  corridor off from the battlements, going on from the rooms' front wall
  (`game-dormitory-corner-2.md`).
- Its door to the corridor opened without a model (`repaint.py doorways`),
  its leaf painted with the common room's own floor.
- Its beds, trunks, bedside tables, armchairs and table, and the beds' quilts
  and sheets, cut where Gemini painted them flat magenta in pictures of the
  dormitory (`game-dormitory-marked-*.md`, `game-dormitory-covers-*.md`); its
  walls by polygons, each edge on its outline.
- Its morning painted whole by Gemini, given the night painting as it is
  (`game-dormitory-day.md`): the same rooms a few hours later, the sky and
  the outside by day, every torch, candle and the fireplace out; laid on the
  painting by its outlines (`tools/art/repaint.py`), and every image of the
  scene cut from it a second time with the same masks.
- Its cells marked by hand and exported by `tools/art/export_scene.py`.
