# Caught

You are almost at the dormitory. Suddenly, Dask steps out of the dark. You are caught.

---

Dask looks very stern. Then, he smiles a little. "I watched you use your heads," he says. "You were very clever. I will let you go, just this once, {wizard}."

---

You learned a lot tonight. You asked a question with a true or false answer, an expression. You made a choice with `if` and `else`, a condition. You learned to repeat with `for`, a loop. You repeated while something is true, with `while`. You lit the dark with `kirra`. You held a moving stair with `osto`. You feel at home in the castle at last. You fall fast asleep.
