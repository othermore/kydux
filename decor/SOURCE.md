# The start screen's and the welcome page's decorations

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later, like the
rest of it (D294).

- `backdrop.webp`: Arvelia on its hill at the right, the village by the lake at the left
  and a calm lake and meadow between them, at dusk: the painting behind the start screen,
  whose page covers its middle. Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/start-backdrop.md`, with two details of the welcome page's painting
  of the castle (`app/public/about/welcome-castle.webp`) as its references, the castle
  and the village, for their shapes, colours and style; the third of three tries, and
  made 1920 pixels wide.
- `ilda.webp`, `orla.webp`, `dask.webp`, `wenna.webp`, `baro.webp`, `eldra.webp`,
  `nuri.webp`, `teku.webp`, `zarek.webp`, `rinlo.webp`, `bimo.webp`: the school's people
  and its cat painted whole, head to feet, each alone, with alpha, in the colours the
  story first gives them. Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/whole-<name>.md`, each with the game's small figure and portrait of
  the same character as its references (the cat with its portrait alone), on a magenta
  ground keyed out by `tools/art/sprites.py whole`, and made 600 pixels tall; each the
  first try.
