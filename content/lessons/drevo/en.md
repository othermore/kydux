# Drevo

The magic word *drevo* lifts the broom you carry into the air! You cast it with your wand, writing `wizard.cast("drevo")`.

Once you are on the broom, the `move_` methods make you fly in that direction. Flying, you can pass over some things, like the hedges, the fences, the benches and the water. And you also go three times as fast as if you were walking. But be careful! You can still bump into the walls and the trees.

If you cast *drevo* again while in the air, the broom sets down wherever your wizard is, as long as the ground can be stood on. Over the water there is nowhere to land, and the program stops.

At the end of the program, if you have not got off the broom, it will set itself down on the ground.
