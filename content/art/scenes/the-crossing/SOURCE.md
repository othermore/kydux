# The crossing

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from its plan on the grid
  (`tools/art/prompts/crossing-painted-3.md`), then edited by it without its grid
  (`crossing-base.md`) and with its walls' gaps painted (`crossing-base-2.md`).
- Its three statues standing in the middle of the gallery, painted by Gemini on that
  picture with the statues taken out of it from its picture without them
  (`crossing-gone.md`), given a picture of where each stands and one of the statues
  alone (`crossing-statues-middle-3.md`): the scene's painting. The statues are cut by
  the difference against the picture they were painted on, the arches against
  Gemini's picture without them (`crossing-gone.md`) (D148); the lower wall's tops are
  boxes on their outlines.
