# The crossing

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from its plan on the grid
  (`tools/art/prompts/crossing-painted-3.md`), then edited by it without its grid
  (`crossing-base.md`) and with its walls' gaps painted (`crossing-base-2.md`): the
  scene's base (D148).
- Its statues and its arches cut by the difference against the picture without them,
  also Gemini's (`crossing-gone.md`); the lower wall's tops by the polygons traced on
  their outlines.
