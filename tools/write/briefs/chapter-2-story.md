Write the two story pages of chapter 2, *The library*, which open and
close it. Chapter 1 ended on the castle road (the closing page is given).
Chapter 2 happens in the castle's library, one place, where the wizard
crosses from the library's door to the wand bank's counter, level by
level. A story page is pages of Markdown separated by `---`, the first
opening with a `#` title, with no code and no Try it. Three or four short
pages each. The same pages in both languages.

1. `content/stories/in-the-castle/<lang>.md`, the opening. Inside the
   castle at last, the new pupils are greeted by Ilda, a teacher. Ilda
   sends the wizard to the wand bank, a counter run by the pupils'
   parents, to borrow a school wand, so that Nuri's spare can go back.
   The way to the wand bank goes through the library. Nuri goes to class.
   At the library's door, the librarian Wenna waits: precise and quiet,
   she loves order and a good question, and she will help the wizard
   across her library, teaching on the way. The last page ends with
   Wenna's turn to teaching, saying that what follows are lessons from
   her.
2. `content/stories/the-wand-bank/<lang>.md`, the closing. Eldra, the
   ghost of the library, has her book back and thanks the wizard,
   whispering, old-fashioned, never frightening. Beyond the library's
   last aisle is the wand bank's counter: the wizard borrows a school
   wand for the year and gives Nuri's spare back (Nuri is there to take
   it). The last page sums up what the chapter taught, each idea with
   the child's word and the programmer's word together: a name for a
   value, a variable; what a thing knows, an attribute, shown with
   `print`; an action that gives something back, like `find_nearest`;
   a key and a door that opens with `door.open()`; and a spell that
   reaches, `fendra`. It ends with night falling over the castle, and
   rumours of the passages after dark.
