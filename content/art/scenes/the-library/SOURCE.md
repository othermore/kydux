# The library

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/image-first-library-6.md`, with the field as its
  style's reference, then edited by it without its grid
  (`game-library6-nogrid.md`).
- Its bottom mended by Gemini from `game-library-margin-3.md`, joined by
  `tools/art/repaint.py`, the painting kept pixel for pixel elsewhere: the
  castle's corridor below the double door, plain pillars in the corners,
  plain wall beside the door.
- The way to the vault ending in an iron safe, painted by Gemini from
  `game-library-vault-2.md` in a box of the painting, joined by
  `tools/art/repaint.py`.
- Its sides painted on by Gemini from `image-first-library-margin.md`, three
  cells on each side, on a canvas made and joined by `tools/art/margin.py`:
  the castle's walls going on, and grass and trees beyond them.
- Its door arches drawn over whoever passes under them, and its walls and
  the little table by Eldra's armchair, cut by polygons, each edge on its
  outline (static things).
- Its counter, bookcases, pile of books, tables, chairs, desk and Eldra's
  book painted flat magenta by Gemini in crops of the painting
  (`game-library-marked-*.md`, each asked three times and voted), and cut
  there with their outlines.
- Each door open, swung in out of sight, its whole doorway the floor of the
  room beyond from Gemini's edit of the doorway alone
  (`game-library-centre-open.md`, `-wenna-open.md`, `-room-open.md`), laid
  in by `tools/art/repaint.py doorways`.
- Each thing that opens or is picked up cut against Gemini's edit without it
  (`game-library-nokeys.md`, `-nobook.md`, `-nooldbook.md`); Eldra's own
  book against the shelf's back carried over it by hand; the armchair, which
  stands in front of whoever is behind it, against Gemini's edit without it
  (`game-library-noarmchair.md`). The two books drawn smaller than painted.
- Its cells marked by hand, cut by `tools/art/image_first.py` and exported by
  `tools/art/export_scene.py`.
