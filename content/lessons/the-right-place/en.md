# The right place

You must be very tired after your journey! But even if you are sleepy, you cannot just sleep anywhere. Some actions can only be done in the right place.

---
## Where to do an action

In this game, the method `wizard.sleep()` lays your wizard down in a free bed, but only when one is right beside you. If you try to sleep anywhere else, your wizard will wonder why you asked, and nothing will happen!

So, a program must first walk to the right place, and then do the action.

---
## Try it

Our own dormitory is through the arch on the right. The arch on the left is the other dormitory, which is not ours.

Let us see what happens if we try to sleep right here in the common room, where there is no bed beside us!
