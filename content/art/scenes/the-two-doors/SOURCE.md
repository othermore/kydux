# The room of two doors

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-two-doors.md`, then edited by it without its
  grid (`game-two-doors-nogrid.md`).
- What it left black at the corners painted by Gemini from
  `game-two-doors-voids.md`, joined by `tools/art/repaint.py`, and what still
  was black tiled from stones of the crossing's picture (`tiled`).
- Each door opened by `tools/art/door.py` from the painting itself, no model asked.
