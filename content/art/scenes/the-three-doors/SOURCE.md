# The corridor of three doors

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-four-doors.md`, then edited by it without its
  grid (`game-three-doors-nogrid.md`).
- What it left black along the top and in the bottom corners painted by Gemini
  from `game-three-doors-voids.md`, joined by `tools/art/repaint.py`, and
  what still was black tiled from the picture's own stones (`tiled`).
- Its three walls moved along their rows by `tools/art/repaint.py spaced`, so
  that the doors lie exactly four cells apart.
- Each door's doorway opened by `tools/art/repaint.py doorways`, and each leaf
  swung open painted by Gemini (`game-three-doors-swung-1.md`, `-2`, `-3`) in
  a box, joined by `tools/art/repaint.py`.
