A pixel art sprite sheet of a young wizard for a children's 2D top-down oblique game, like classic handheld RPG characters: a child in a blue robe and a pointed blue hat with a gold band, friendly face, chibi proportions. On a flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, a strict grid of 4 rows and 3 columns, every frame the same size and the character the same size and place in every frame. Row 1: facing down (towards the viewer); row 2: facing left; row 3: facing right; row 4: facing up (back to the viewer). Column 1: standing still; columns 2 and 3: the two steps of a walk cycle. Crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, a limited palette, no text, no shadows outside the character.
