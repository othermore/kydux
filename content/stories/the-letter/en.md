# The letter

A little paper bird flies in through your window. It lands on your hand and unfolds into a letter with a heavy wax seal. The letter is for you. You open it and read:

"We invite you to your first year at Arvelia. We teach magic in an old castle on a hill above a deep lake. Come to us before autumn begins."

It is signed by Orla, the head of the school.

---

You pack your bags and travel far from home. The road is long and dusty. Finally, you reach a small village at the foot of a tall hill. High above the village, the stone towers of Arvelia touch the sky. The sun begins to set. It paints the clouds pink and gold.

---

You walk up the hill alone in the dusk. At the top, a great iron gate blocks the path. The gate is shut tight. It has no handle and no bell. You have no wand yet. You do not know any magic. But the old iron gate is very quiet. It seems to be listening. Maybe it waits for a secret word.

---

How will you open the gate? The story is about to begin. But first: who are you?
