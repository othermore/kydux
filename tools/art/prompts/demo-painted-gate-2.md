Repaint the first image, keeping its composition exactly: it is a precise map of a game level on a grid of square tiles, and the game will place characters on those tiles, so every shape must stay exactly where it is, with the same outline, the same size and the same position in the frame. Only its flat colours are to be painted over with texture and light. The second image is only a reference for brushwork, outlines and colours; it shows a different place, so do not copy its layout, its walls, its path or its hedges.

What each flat colour is in the first image: green is a lawn; light brown is a dirt path, exactly in the zigzag shape shown; light grey-beige is a narrow flagstone passage floor between walls; the large flat mid-grey areas are the tops of thick stone walls, seen from above, paved with capstones; the dark grey bands below them are the walls' vertical front faces of fitted stone blocks; the dark green block with its darker band is one row of three round trimmed hedge bushes; the thin brown bars are low wooden fences with posts, each bar a run of fence; the small grey oval is a flat stepping stone. The black bands at the top and bottom stay black.

Paint in the style of the second image: bold dark outlines, warm saturated colours, soft painted shading, lit tops and shaded faces. Add nothing: no trees, buildings, doors, gates, people, animals, flowers on the paths, or text. A few small flowers and tufts on the lawn only.
