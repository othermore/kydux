# Keys and doors

You already know that a door is an object with its own method, `door.open()`, and that it only opens for a wizard right beside it. But some doors are locked with a key, and a locked door will only open when you carry its key!

---
## Hidden things

Sometimes you cannot see a thing on the floor, but it is there all the same for the program.

Your method `find_nearest` finds it wherever it lies, and `reveal` shows it through whatever hides it, as you learned.
