# The room of two doors

Kydux's own art, made for the game and under the GNU GPL, version 3 or
later, like the rest of it.

- Painted by Gemini 3 Pro Image (`gemini-3-pro-image`) from
  `tools/art/prompts/scene-two-doors.md`, then edited by it: without its grid
  (`game-two-doors-nogrid.md`), the round of the castle painted
  (`game-two-doors-voids.md`).
- Its base is Gemini's edit without the doors (`two-doors-base.md`); the doors shut
  (`two-doors-closed.md`) and open (`two-doors-open.md`) are Gemini's edits of it, the
  right door shut hung on the jamb it opens on (`two-doors-closed-hinged.md`), and
  each leaf the difference (D148). So are the arches, from `two-doors-gone.md`.
