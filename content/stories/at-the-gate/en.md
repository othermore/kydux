# At the gate

You stand at the great iron gate. The gate is shut. You do not know how to open it.

---

Another child walks up the path.

"Hello! I am Nuri. I am a first-year pupil like you. I grew up among wizards. I already know some magic."

---

Nuri smiles at you.

"Do not worry, {wizard}. The gate listens to instructions. I will show you how to open it right here. A good wizard investigates first. We try, we watch what happens, and we try again."
