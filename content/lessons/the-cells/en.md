# The cells

This map I gave you is the naughty child's map. It is a plan of the whole castle, and it always shows us what we need to see at that moment, if we say the right spell.

It works with a system of coordinates: each cell on the plan is named by two numbers. The first is x and the second is y, and they are written (x, y). The x counts from the left to the right, and the y from the top to the bottom. And, very importantly: they start at 0.

So the top left cell will always be (0, 0). The one just to its right is (1, 0) and the one just below it is (0, 1).

---
## Counting the steps

The important thing is that those numbers tell us how many steps there are between two cells. For example, between (0, 0) and (2, 0) there are two steps. A step to the right adds one to x. A step down adds one to y. If we take a step to the left we subtract one from x, and if we take a step up we subtract one from y.

So to go from cell (5, 12) to (9, 9), your wizard has to walk four steps right and three up. You only have to count how many steps there are from one number to another.

---
## The map's word

The map only works with the word in its corner, *orvim*.

Cast with the wand, it opens the plan of this corridor, with our three figures on it. It stays open until you close it.
